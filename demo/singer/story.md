# Root–microbe interface in agar

A synchrotron microtomography reconstruction collected at the Advanced Light Source
(beamline 8.3.2). The specimen is a pipette-mounted agar column ("YES agar", wet
preparation) imaged in 2020 as part of a rhizosphere study.

The volume is an 8-bit reconstruction cropped to a region of interest: 1004 slices of
521 x 816 voxels. Brightness tracks X-ray attenuation, so denser material — cell walls,
mineral inclusions — reads bright, while pore space and water read dark.

---

## What to look for

Sweep the transfer function to isolate the dense network threading the agar. At low
thresholds the agar matrix itself fills the view; raise it and the structure separates
into discrete filaments and voids.

---

## Why VR

Filament connectivity is genuinely hard to judge from orthogonal slices — the eye has to
integrate across hundreds of 2D planes. Stereo viewing with head-coupled parallax makes
"does this branch connect?" a one-second question instead of a ten-minute one. That is
the whole argument for ascribe-web.
